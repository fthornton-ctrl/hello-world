I used AI assistance to review my portfolio code, refine visual design, and implement accessibility improvements to meet WCAG AA standards. First, I updated the navigation color palette because light text on the original teal background presented a contrast concern. I selected a deeper dark teal (#075B5B) to ensure a 7.8:1 contrast ratio, making the labels much easier to read. Second, I implemented a "Skip to main content" link on every page, allowing keyboard users to bypass repeated navigation links and jump directly to page content. Third, I strengthened keyboard focus indicators with a high-contrast 3px outline and added aria-current="page" to active navigation links. 

Finally, I built an accessible contact form on the About/Contact page using semantic <fieldset> and <legend> elements, explicit <label> tags, and accessible error handling with aria-describedby and role="alert". This project taught me that web accessibility is fundamental to user experience. Designing with Gestalt principles like proximity and common region creates visually organized, intuitive interfaces that benefit all users regardless of their abilities or devices.
